# NOTICE

wow-everlake-world-data holds update files for Everlake's world database (a WoW Forever realm running a TrinityCore
fork), generated by `tools/forever-data` in [wow-project-everlake](https://github.com/buildthehomelab/wow-project-everlake)
from public data about the live game. The files are licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (see LICENSE),
because several of the sources below are GPL-3.0.

Every row in the files names the sources that gave it. Each slice's `manifest.json` lists the pins (commits, builds and
dates) of every source it was made from.

## Sources

- **EG Link** by Efficient Games: Data from Efficient Games (https://efficient.games), licensed under CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed: voted against the other sources and written as TrinityCore
  rows. Used for: quest progress and completion texts, quest records, objectives; loot drops and their chances
  (slice 2: drops per loot window); trainer spells and vendor stock (slice 3: trainer and vendor windows);
  live sightings that confirm spawn points (slice 4); new NPCs' and objects' templates (slice 5: the client's own query
  data as EG Link records it), their levels, the reactions players saw, their services and stock, and chest loot.
- **forever-db** from Polestar (https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar): GPL-3.0 for its tables. Used for: quest givers
  and enders, objectives (checked against); the client item list loot items are checked against; its drop rows
  (`item_source`, which Polestar builds from pfQuest/VMaNGOS and CMaNGOS Classic data) as Classic drop chances, each
  such row marked `classic` in its note; spawn points (slices 4 and 5: QuestieDB's points, CMaNGOS heights); NPC levels
  where EG Link has none (slice 5).
- **QuestieDB** (https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB), including player-recorded QuestieTrace corrections: GPL-3.0.
  Changed: voted against the other sources and written as TrinityCore rows. Used for: quest givers and enders; loot
  drops players recorded (QuestieTrace item traces).
- **ForeverDB.net** (https://foreverdb.net): Data from ForeverDB.net, GPL-3.0. Used in the vote on quest givers and enders,
  and as the second witness for vendor stock (`2026_11_12_230_world_everlake_norgannondb_vendor_stock.sql`: only vendors
  its players saw on Forever).
- **NorgannonDB** by Maro (https://github.com/zmarotrix/NorgannonDB), GPL-3.0. Changed: compared with the world and
  ForeverDB.net, written as TrinityCore rows. Used for: vendor stock and drops (`2026_11_12_230_world_everlake_norgannondb_vendor_stock.sql`,
  `2026_11_12_231_world_everlake_norgannondb_drops.sql`).
  NorgannonDB compiles QuestieDB (GPL-3.0), pfQuest by Shagu (https://github.com/shagu/pfQuest; MIT, Copyright (c)
  2017-2021 Eric Mauser (Shagu)), DungeonJournal by Exehn (used with the author's permission, on condition of this
  credit), cmangos classic-db (GPL-3.0) and its Collector players' recordings; its NOTICE.md lists them.
- **Icy Veins' WoW Forever database** (https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/): what players' addons recorded on live
  Forever. Used (the project's decision of 2026-11-12) as the second witness for NorgannonDB's vendor stock and drops
  in files 230 and 231, and for those drops' chances and the stock's prices.
- **TrinityCore `forever` branch** (https://github.com/advocaite/TrinityCore/tree/forever), GPL-2.0: the world
  the files are written against; its Forever beta sniffs vote too. Slice 5 takes a new NPC's faction, speeds and
  attack times from the fork's NPCs nearby, each row's note naming them.
- Heights of spawn points that have none (slice 4) are read from Everlake's own maps, extracted from the game client.
- Forever's client tables, through wago.tools (https://wago.tools), were used as references only: slice 5 checks that a
  model is in the client and reads the client's faction tables to work out how an NPC reacts to players.
- Wowhead's public tooltips and the item history of ericbtaylorjr/forever-db were used as references only; nothing is
  copied from them (slice 3 checks against the item history that a trainer's spells exist in the client).

The world build's files (`*_world_everlake_build.sql`) are generated by the ForeverTracker
(https://github.com/buildthehomelab/wow-forever-tracker) from EG Link (quest and creature records as the server sent
them, vendor windows, what quest NPCs say at the turn-in) and QuestieDB (quest givers and enders, vendor lists, the
classes a quest is for). Each file's header and its manifest in `build/` name the pins.

The Forge exports (`*_900_world_everlake_forge_<operation>_*.sql`) are rows the ForeverTracker's Forge
(https://github.com/buildthehomelab/wow-forever-tracker) added to Everlake's test realm and that were tried there
first, exported as they stood on that realm. Each row's `everlake_import_rows` note and each file's header name what
gave it:

- operations 3, 4, 6 and 7: the official client's own tables (skill lines and spell ranks, the Map table) and the
  Forge's rules (a trainer's price and level from its other spells); no outside source.
- operation 13: EG Link's trainer windows (CC BY 4.0, credited above).
- operations 14 and 15: ForeverDB.net (Data from ForeverDB.net, GPL-3.0) as the single source: a quest giver, what
  vendors sold and what dropped, as its players saw it on live Forever.
- operation 16: NorgannonDB (GPL-3.0, credited above), each row kept only where a live Forever recording (EG Link,
  ForeverDB.net or Icy Veins) or AtlasLoot Classic Forever also lists it.

# NOTICE

wow-everlake-world-data holds update files for Everlake's world database (a WoW Forever realm running a TrinityCore
fork), generated by `tools/forever-data` in [wow-project-everlake](https://github.com/buildthehomelab/wow-project-everlake)
from public data about the live game. The files are licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (see LICENSE),
because several of the sources below are GPL-3.0.

Every row in the files names the sources that gave it. Each slice's `manifest.json` lists the pins (commits, builds and
dates) of every source it was made from.

## Sources

- **EG Link** by Efficient Games: Data from Efficient Games (https://efficient.games), licensed under CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed: voted against the other sources and written as TrinityCore
  rows. Used for: quest progress and completion texts, quest records, objectives; loot drops and their chances
  (slice 2: drops per loot window); trainer spells and vendor stock (slice 3: trainer and vendor windows);
  live sightings that confirm spawn points (slice 4); new NPCs' and objects' templates (slice 5: the client's own query
  data as EG Link records it), their levels, the reactions players saw, their services and stock, and chest loot.
- **forever-db** from Polestar (https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar): GPL-3.0 for its tables. Used for: quest givers
  and enders, objectives (checked against); the client item list loot items are checked against; its drop rows
  (`item_source`, which Polestar builds from pfQuest/VMaNGOS and CMaNGOS Classic data) as Classic drop chances, each
  such row marked `classic` in its note; spawn points (slices 4 and 5: QuestieDB's points, CMaNGOS heights); NPC levels
  where EG Link has none (slice 5).
- **QuestieDB** (https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB), including player-recorded QuestieTrace corrections: GPL-3.0.
  Changed: voted against the other sources and written as TrinityCore rows. Used for: quest givers and enders; loot
  drops players recorded (QuestieTrace item traces).
- **ForeverDB.net** (https://foreverdb.net): Data from ForeverDB.net, GPL-3.0. Used in the vote on quest givers and enders,
  and as the second witness for vendor stock (`2026_11_12_230_world_everlake_norgannondb_vendor_stock.sql`: only vendors
  its players saw on Forever).
- **NorgannonDB** by Maro (https://github.com/zmarotrix/NorgannonDB), GPL-3.0. Changed: compared with the world and
  ForeverDB.net, written as TrinityCore rows. Used for: vendor stock and drops (`2026_11_12_230_world_everlake_norgannondb_vendor_stock.sql`,
  `2026_11_12_231_world_everlake_norgannondb_drops.sql`).
  NorgannonDB compiles QuestieDB (GPL-3.0), pfQuest by Shagu (https://github.com/shagu/pfQuest; MIT, Copyright (c)
  2017-2021 Eric Mauser (Shagu)), DungeonJournal by Exehn (used with the author's permission, on condition of this
  credit), cmangos classic-db (GPL-3.0) and its Collector players' recordings; its NOTICE.md lists them.
- **Icy Veins' WoW Forever database** (https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/): what players' addons recorded on live
  Forever. Used (the project's decision of 2026-11-12) as the second witness for NorgannonDB's vendor stock and drops
  in files 230 and 231, and for those drops' chances and the stock's prices.
- **ForeverChanges** (https://foreverchanges.pro): what the players of its addon saw on live Forever, shown on the
  site over a base of Classic's data. The site states no licence, and its terms ask that its compiled data not be
  republished wholesale as one's own; it is credited here as the source of the rows named below, used by the project's
  decision of 2026-11-14. Only what its players saw is taken, row by row, for things the realm lacked.
- **cmangos classic-db** (https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db), GPL-3.0: the content database of the original
  game (patch 1.12), not of WoW: Forever. Used, by the project's decision of 2026-11-14, as the last source: only
  for profession recipes that Forever kept and that nobody could learn on the realm, where it names the trainer
  that taught them.
- **AtlasLoot Classic Forever** (https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/atlasloot-forever), GPL-2.0: the loot browser
  most Forever players run. Its tables name the creature or chest that drops each item; only ids and chances are
  taken.
- **TrinityCore `forever` branch** (https://github.com/advocaite/TrinityCore/tree/forever), GPL-2.0: the world
  the files are written against; its Forever beta sniffs vote too. Slice 5 takes a new NPC's faction, speeds and
  attack times from the fork's NPCs nearby, each row's note naming them.
- **TrinityCore** (https://github.com/TrinityCore/TrinityCore), GPL-2.0: `2026_11_16_920_world_everlake_aq20_andorov.sql`
  takes Lieutenant General Andorov's and his Kaldorei Elites' spawns and formation in Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj from
  TrinityCore's update for the script that runs them (2026_02_04_00_world), set to this realm's AQ20 difficulty.
  `2026_11_16_921_world_everlake_aq20_rajaxx_waves.sql` gives General Rajaxx's waves the tags that update gives them
  (GeneralRajaxxWave1 to 7), on this realm's own spawns of those waves.
- Heights of spawn points that have none (slice 4) are read from Everlake's own maps, extracted from the game client.
- `2026_11_18_920_world_everlake_tashkal.sql` (Ta'shkal, Mount Hyjal's hidden cat) is written by hand from Wowhead's
  WoW Forever database (https://www.wowhead.com/forever, credited with operation 27): the ids, level, family, models,
  attack speed and abilities of npc=268231, the map points of object=660408 (Lonely Lantern) and object=660432
  (Corrupted Cat Figurine), and how the two are used, from the pages' comments; no text of the pages is copied. Its
  X and Y come from those points through the client's UiMapAssignment (wago.tools), its heights from Everlake's own
  maps, and Ta'shkal's stats and faction from the fork's Frostsaber Stalker (7432).
- `2026_11_18_922_world_everlake_trainer_ranks.sql` (Holy Shield 4, Death Mark 2 to 4, Shank 2 at the class trainers) is
  written by hand from Wowhead's WoW Forever database (https://www.wowhead.com/forever, credited with operation 27):
  that the class trainers teach them, their level and their training cost, from spell=1239570, 1325756, 1325757,
  1325758 and 1329549; no text of the pages is copied. The rank each one requires comes from the client's
  SkillLineAbility (wago.tools); the trainers are those of the realm that already teach the rank before (Holy Shield 3)
  or Sinister Strike 2.
- `2026_11_18_923_world_everlake_sfk_silver_ingot.sql` (the Enchanted Silver Ingot in Shadowfang Keep) is written by
  hand from Wowhead's WoW Forever database (https://www.wowhead.com/forever, credited with operation 27): that
  object=629065 lies on the table next to Archmage Arugal, from the page's comments; no text of the page is copied.
  Its point is read from Everlake's own vmaps, extracted from the game client.
- `2026_11_18_924_world_everlake_kobold_tracks.sql` (Kobold Tracks in Elwynn Forest, one credit per track) is written by
  hand from player reports and the fork's own goober code; it uses no outside source.
- `2026_11_18_926_world_everlake_quest_drop_sources.sql` (new Forever creatures and chests whose loot is a quest item)
  is written by a script from EG Link (CC BY 4.0, credited above): the creatures' and objects' templates as the live
  server sent them to players' clients, their levels, the reactions players saw, where players killed or opened them
  and the quest items' measured chances; QuestieDB (credited above): which creature or object drops each quest item,
  and spawn points; forever-db from Polestar (credited above): spawn points. As slice 5 does, a creature's faction,
  speeds and attack times come from the realm's own NPCs nearby whose faction fits the reactions players saw, and the
  client's faction tables say which fit. Heights are read from Everlake's own maps and vmaps. For 20 creatures EG Link
  has no record of, the name, title, type, family, classification and models come from **DatabaseForever**
  (https://github.com/EmuForever/DatabaseForever, the EmuForever project's world database, used by the project's
  decision of 2026-11-18; its repository states no licence, and it is credited here); its other values for them look
  generated and are not taken.
- Forever's client tables, through wago.tools (https://wago.tools), were used as references only: slice 5 checks that a
  model is in the client and reads the client's faction tables to work out how an NPC reacts to players.
- Wowhead's public tooltips and the item history of ericbtaylorjr/forever-db were used as references only in the
  slices and the world build; nothing is copied from them there (slice 3 checks against the item history that a
  trainer's spells exist in the client). Wowhead is a source of rows in the Forge exports named below.

The world build's files (`*_world_everlake_build.sql`) are generated by the ForeverTracker
(https://github.com/buildthehomelab/wow-forever-tracker) from EG Link (quest and creature records as the server sent
them, vendor windows, what quest NPCs say at the turn-in) and QuestieDB (quest givers and enders, vendor lists, the
classes a quest is for). Each file's header and its manifest in `build/` name the pins. From
`2026_11_18_950` on, the Forever quests the realm lacked are added in slices (a zone or a chain at a time): the same
export code writes each slice's file from the same two sources, checked against the test realm's world.

The Forge exports (`*_900_world_everlake_forge_<operation>_*.sql`) are rows the ForeverTracker's Forge
(https://github.com/buildthehomelab/wow-forever-tracker) added to Everlake's test realm and that were tried there
first, exported as they stood on that realm. Each row's `everlake_import_rows` note and each file's header name what
gave it:

- operations 3, 4, 6 and 7: the official client's own tables (skill lines and spell ranks, the Map table) and the
  Forge's rules (a trainer's price and level from its other spells); no outside source.
- operation 13: EG Link's trainer windows (CC BY 4.0, credited above).
- operations 14 and 15: ForeverDB.net (Data from ForeverDB.net, GPL-3.0) as the single source: a quest giver, what
  vendors sold and what dropped, as its players saw it on live Forever.
- operation 16: NorgannonDB (GPL-3.0, credited above), each row kept only where a live Forever recording (EG Link,
  ForeverDB.net or Icy Veins) or AtlasLoot Classic Forever also lists it.
- operation 18: EG Link's trainer windows (CC BY 4.0, credited above). A pet trainer's rank takes its level and the
  rank it needs from the official pet trainer list (trainer 273), as the core does for the ranks those trainers
  already sold.
- operations 19 and 21: Icy Veins' WoW Forever database (credited above) as the single source: what vendors sold,
  what chests and creatures dropped and how often, as its players' addons recorded it on live Forever. Only ids and
  measured chances are taken. Left out on purpose: its trainer lists (they offer every pet ability at pet and hunter
  trainers, which Forever teaches by taming) and the Waylaid Crate and Sealed Artisan's Writ drops (world drops it
  lists creature by creature from small samples).
- operation 20: Wowhead's WoW Forever database (https://www.wowhead.com/forever): the NPC a quest's summary sends the
  player back to, taken as that quest's ender where exactly one spawned creature has that name. Only the two ids are
  written; no text of the page is copied. The project's decision of 2026-11-14: Wowhead, a public copy of what
  players recorded, is the main source of truth, ahead of QuestieDB and Icy Veins.
- operation 22: EG Link's quest records (CC BY 4.0, credited above), for quests the world already had: each quest as
  the live server sent it to a client, read again from the newest client build that recorded it. Where that record
  is from a later build than the row and says something else, the row takes it: titles and texts, reputation (compared
  in points, whether a row gives it as a tier or as an override), the XP difficulty and multiplier, a battleground's
  currency, the level a quest is offered from. The texts are the server's own, as the game sends them. Each statement
  names the value it replaces, so it changes nothing where a row was changed since, and nothing when it runs again.
  Not taken: money and a quest's own level (the server sends those for the player who asked), a value the record does
  not state, and the fields that decide how the server handles a quest (type, flags, zone, races, the next quest).
- operation 23: ForeverChanges (credited above), for what the realm lacked: what an NPC says when a quest is handed
  in and while it is not done yet, for 254 quest windows that had no text; 26 creature drops players counted at least
  twice in at least ten kills (recipes and quest items; an item fifteen or more creatures drop is a world drop and
  was left out); one item at six vendors. The texts are the game's own. The site shows them as a player's client did
  and marks the player's name, class and race: those marks are written back as `$N`, `$C` and `$R`, and a text that
  might still hold a player's own name was left out. Where EG Link (CC BY 4.0, credited above) recorded the same
  window, its wording is the one written. The site names NPCs and places them on a map: each was matched to the
  realm's creature of that name standing there, and a name that could not be told apart was left out.
- operation 26: cmangos classic-db (credited above), for recipes Forever kept that nobody on the realm taught: the
  original game's trainers of Goldthorn Tea (Henry Stern) and of Heavy Mageweave, Runecloth and Heavy Runecloth
  Bandage (the two Triage doctors, Doctor Gregory Victor and Doctor Gustaf VanHowzen). The three already stood in
  the world without a trainer list: each gets one of its own, numbered 1,000,000 plus the creature's number, the
  link to it and the trainer flags (`npcflag` 16 and 64, added only where missing), with the original game's cost,
  skill and level for each recipe. Recipes Forever removed (the old healing potions) and recipes the original game
  taught through a line of conversation (a script, not a row) were left out.
- operation 27: Wowhead's WoW Forever database (https://www.wowhead.com/forever), read from its pages (item pages:
  *Dropped by*, *Contained in*, *Sold by*) slowly in a browser tab by the tracker's page crawler, which keeps only ids
  and players' counts and never a page's text. Used for what the realm lacked, for items other content waits for (a
  quest's objective, a recipe, a mount) and new items: 13 creature drops players counted at least twice in at least
  ten kills, the chance being drops over kills (an item fifteen or more creatures drop is a world drop and was left
  out); 5 quest objects that gave their item every time they were opened; 4 vendor rows sold for money only. Wowhead
  states no licence for its data; it is credited here, and only ids and measured counts are taken.
- operation 28: Icy Veins' WoW Forever database (credited above) as the single source, for the recipe items players
  could not get whose recipes nobody could learn (patterns, plans, formulas and schematics, most of them new in
  Forever): 81 creature drops, as its players' addons recorded them on live Forever, with the measured chance (0.32 to
  9.09 %). Most are one creature seen dropping the item; five glove patterns and plans list the bosses of Zul'Farrak,
  Maraudon, the Sunken Temple, Uldaman and Blackrock Depths that dropped them. Only ids and measured chances are
  taken. Applied on the test realm as the tracker's Forge operation 27.
- operations 29 to 32: cmangos classic-db (credited above), what the original game spawned only while a holiday ran,

  Elders, and 6 objects), the Feast of Winter Veil (30: The Abominable Greench, Seersa Copperpinch, Guchie
  Jinglepocket and 4 objects), Hallow's End (31: the Wickerman, its Guardian, Sergeant Hartman, the Southshore Stink
  Bomb Counter and 4 objects) and Children's Week (32: Alowicious Czervik). Each spawn with its link to the realm's

  Applied on the test realm as the tracker's Forge operation 40.
